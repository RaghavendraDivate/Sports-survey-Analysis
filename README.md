# Sports Survey Insights

A Power BI dashboard analyzing survey data on sports preferences and participation, collected from a survey conducted among college students.

## Overview

This project explores responses from a sports preferences and participation survey conducted among college students, covering competitive sports participation, career aspirations in sports, favorite sports by gender, role models, and motivations for playing sports — visualized as an interactive multi-panel dashboard.

## Key Insights

- **Participation**: 68.33% of respondents have participated in tournaments at a competitive level; 60% do not aspire to a sports career.
- **Sports Importance**: Majority of respondents "Strongly Agree" or "Agree" that sports are important.
- **Favorite Sport by Gender**: Cricket leads among males (44.44%), Badminton leads among females (43.75%).
- **Role Models**: Virat Kohli is the top role model across both genders (48–50%), followed by PV Sindhu (female) and MS Dhoni (male).
- **Motivations**: The most common combination is "Health, Competition, Fun, Socializing" (18.18%), followed by "Health, Competition, Fun" (16.36%).
- **Facility Ratings**: A significant share of respondents rate facilities as "Poor" or "Fair" — roughly 60% combined across both competitive and non-competitive groups — while only 20% (competitive) and 5.56% (non-competitive) rate them "Excellent."

## Recommendations

- **Facility investment is a priority area**: with "Poor" + "Fair" ratings covering the majority of responses on both sides of the competitive/non-competitive split, facility quality is a consistent pain point worth addressing before other engagement initiatives.
- **Address the participation-to-career gap**: despite 68.33% having competed at a tournament level, 60% don't see sports as a career path — suggesting a lack of visible career pathways or support structures, worth investigating further (e.g., a follow-up survey on *why*).
- **Leverage high sports-importance sentiment**: since most respondents already agree sports matter, engagement efforts (events, awareness) can build on existing goodwill rather than needing to first convince people of relevance.
- **Sport-specific facility investment**: given Cricket and Badminton dominate preference by gender, prioritizing infrastructure for these sports specifically may yield the highest participation return.
